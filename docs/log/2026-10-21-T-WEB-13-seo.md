## What was built

Every page has its own title and description, a canonical URL on `https://healthcare.bytespate.com` and `hreflang` alternates for Bangla, English and `x-default` (Bangla). While the demo notice is on, pages carry `noindex, nofollow` and `robots.txt` disallows everything; with real content it allows the site and still excludes `/staff`. `sitemap.xml` lists every page (including all doctors, departments, centres, packages, branches and articles) in both languages. The locale proxy now leaves `/staff` paths alone, so on the shared host they reach the staff app.

## Decisions applied

- Indexing follows the demo notice flag in `site.json`: switching the notice off for a real hospital also makes the site indexable.
- Structured data (FR-WEB-SEO-003) is not emitted in WEB-A: it is only allowed for real content.

## How to debug

- Wrong canonical: the path passed to `withAlternates()` in that page's `generateMetadata`.
- Pages indexed by mistake: check `notice.enabled` in `content/site.json`.
